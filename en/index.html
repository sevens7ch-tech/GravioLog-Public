FEATURES

Everything the driver needs on the road – in one app.

Laid out like a real job sheet, only faster: sections fold open, pick lists remember your entries.

Transport order

Header data and as many trips as you need. Vehicles, trailers, equipment and services from pick lists – one PDF page per trip.

Handover report

The customer signs right on the phone. The signature lands in the right place on the job sheet.

Address straight to Google Maps

One tap on the map icon at the loading or unloading point opens the address in Google Maps right away – for example to plan the route there straight away.

Photos with the order

Load, damage, the situation on site – take a photo and attach it straight to the order.

Working hours, also via Wi-Fi

Record working hours – automatically if you like, as soon as the phone detects the company Wi-Fi.

Leave request

Request leave right from the app – as a clean PDF with your letterhead.

PDF builder

Design the job sheet yourself: sections on/off, order, headings, accent colour and your own note.

Orders from dispatch

With GravioLOG Public Dispo – for licence holders – create an order on the PC and send it encrypted via WhatsApp link to the driver’s phone.

Multilingual

App and PDF set separately: German, English, Italian, Dutch, French, Spanish.

Voice input – offline or with Google

Speak instead of typing: the microphone in every text field turns speech into text. By default with Whisper right on the phone – 100 % offline, no recording goes to the internet, to a cloud service or to Google. Google speech recognition can be chosen instead; the recording is then sent to Google for recognition and voice input is no longer offline.

The Whisper speech model (about 180 MB) is downloaded once, automatically over Wi-Fi. After that, offline voice input works without internet.

Offline – and what needs internet

Without a connection: Fill in the order, get it signed, attach photos, create the PDF, track working hours and use voice input with Whisper.

With internet: Sending via WhatsApp or e-mail, opening orders from dispatch, updates, the one-time download of the speech model, activating with the licence key and a short licence check about every 30 days, the dispatch web app in the browser and – if selected – Google speech recognition.

GUIDE

From installation to your first order.

Step by step – how to get GravioLOG Public ready in your business.

GravioLOG Public manual (PDF, German) GravioLOG Public Dispo manual (PDF, German)

1 · Install the app

  1. On the Android phone, tap “Download APK” below.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, turn on “Allow from this source”.
  4. Tap “Install” and open GravioLOG Public.

2 · Set up your company

  1. Menu (⋮ top right) → Company details & logo: enter logo, company name and footer.
  2. Enter dispatch’s WhatsApp number – every job sheet will go there.
  3. Choose the language of the app and of the PDF.

3 · Adjust lists & fields

  1. Menu → Lists & fields: add your own vehicles, trailers and equipment.
  2. Hide fields you don’t need, add your own pick lists.
  3. Tip: vehicles with “Kran” (crane) in the name automatically show loader crane work.

4 · Design the job sheet

  1. Menu → PDF builder: switch sections on or off and reorder them.
  2. Set the accent colour, document title and your own note.
  3. Check the preview with the eye icon at the top.

5 · Fill in your first order

  1. Enter the header data or open the order from dispatch via its WhatsApp link.
  2. Fill in the trip: times, waiting time, services, photos.
  3. Tap the signature field and let the customer sign.

6 · Send & activate

  1. Green send icon: the job sheet goes to dispatch as a PDF – or pick the customer in WhatsApp or send it by e-mail.
  2. Every trip automatically lands in the Trip archive (Menu → Archive), where you can find it any time.
  3. After the trial, enter the licence key from the e-mail in the menu under Activate (internet needed once). The app applies renewals automatically.
